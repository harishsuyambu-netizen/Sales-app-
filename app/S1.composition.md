## Composition and regions

- **S1.R1 [R9, A1] — Persistent navigation (fixed chrome).** Keep the shared CRM identity and five destinations in source order. Dashboard is selected on initial entry; the selected destination follows subsequent navigation. At narrow widths this becomes the shared horizontally scrolling row and stays above content.
- **S1.R2 [R1] — Page heading (content-sized).** In the main area, place `Sales dashboard` first and the subtitle `Pipeline, leads and what to do today` directly below it. The 5 px gradient strip is above both navigation and page content.
- **S1.R3 [R1] — Four KPI cards (responsive row).** Immediately below the heading, arrange Open pipeline, Weighted forecast, Won revenue, and Lead conversion in that order. Each card gives the value highest emphasis and the related count/subtext second. Open pipeline uses the source blue-to-navy highlight; other cards use white. Open pipeline is value of deals before Won plus open-deal count; forecast is value × stage win chance; Won revenue includes closed-deal count; conversion is converted leads divided by all leads, rounded to a whole percent, with New-lead count. Format every amount in Indian currency style. At 1366 px, use four equal columns. At narrow widths, wrap to two columns where space allows and one column at 320 px without clipping labels or values.
- **S1.R4 [R1] — Pipeline by stage (left evidence card).** In the first two-column evidence row, show the four stage rows in Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Won order. Each row groups stage and count on the left, total value on the right, with a proportional bar below/alongside based on the largest stage total. Stage name and numeric value remain visible when color is unavailable.
- **S1.R5 [R1] — Leads by source (right evidence card).** Pair the stage card at wide width. Sort sources by descending lead count; show each source, count, and proportional bar. Preserve readable source text and counts; bars are supplementary evidence, not the only label.
- **S1.R6 [R1] — Today’s activities (left follow-up card).** In the second two-column row, show the count of incomplete activities due Today and rows with type, title, company, and time. If none are due, replace the rows with the exact message `All clear for today.` Keep this task-oriented list compact but do not omit any of those four row fields.
- **S1.R7 [R1] — Top open deals (right follow-up card).** Pair with today’s activities at wide width. Show up to four open deals, sorted by value descending. Every row groups owner initials, deal name, company, stage, and formatted value. Do not include Won deals. Keep owner initials and stage text readable independently of decorative color.
- **S1.R8 [R10] — Success feedback (shared transient region).** When a downstream workflow returns with success feedback, show its exact message in the shared bottom-center position for about 2.2 seconds; it must not obscure the KPI or the next interaction.

## Spatial behavior and states

The main content begins after the shared navigation and 26 px inset. At 1366 × 768, R3 occupies one compact four-card row; R4/R5 and R6/R7 form two successive two-column rows with consistent 24 px section gaps and 18 px card insets. Keep title, KPIs, and the beginning of both evidence rows reachable before substantial scrolling. R3 is content-sized; evidence cards are content-sized. The page content owns all vertical scrolling, bounded by the fixed top strip/navigation; the dashboard has no independent vertical list scroll. At widths below 900 px, stack R4 before R5 and R6 before R7 in that reading order, as well as wrapping the KPIs. Narrow widths preserve every metric, source, stage and deal field; vertical scrolling is preferable to compressed unreadable columns.

## Reading, focus, and accessibility

Read/focus order: Dashboard navigation item and remaining navigation destinations; page title and subtitle; KPI cards left-to-right; Pipeline by stage rows top-to-bottom; Leads by source rows top-to-bottom; Today’s activities rows top-to-bottom; Top open deals rows top-to-bottom; then any reachable navigation destination. The summary cards are informational; do not imply they are actions. Maintain text labels for all plotted bars and provide a textual value/count for every KPI. Ensure status and stage are textually named, sufficient contrast is maintained, and keyboard focus is visible on navigation. Announce refreshed or empty activity content meaningfully; `All clear for today.` is the explicit empty state, not a blank card.
