## Composition and regions

- **S3.R1 [R9, A1] — Persistent navigation (fixed chrome).** Use shared navigation with Pipeline selected. Preserve the five destinations and wide/narrow behaviors.
- **S3.R2 [R5] — Page heading.** Show `Deals pipeline` at the top of the main content area under the shared top strip.
- **S3.R3 [R5] — Open pipeline summary.** Immediately below the heading, give open-deal count, open value, and weighted forecast in one compact, text-first line, in that order. Keep the summary visible before the board and refresh it when a deal moves.
- **S3.R4 [R5] — Four-stage board.** Place the main board beneath the summary, with lanes in exact left-to-right order: Qualified (30%), Proposal (50%), Negotiation (75%), Won (100%). At 1366 px, show four balanced columns together. Each lane begins with stage name and chance, then count and total value, then its deals. Use the source panel fill and 16 px radius; stage identity and chance are text, not color-only signals.
- **S3.R5 [R5, A6] — Deal cards and movement.** Within the appropriate lane, show deal name first, company next, formatted value and owner initials together, then Back and Next actions. Each action moves one stage only. Back is unavailable at Qualified; Next is unavailable at Won. The unavailable direction is visibly and semantically disabled, not merely removed without explanation. Movement refreshes lane counts/values and the summary. Moving into Won displays exact `Deal won: {deal name}` feedback in the shared bottom-center position for about 2.2 seconds.
- **S3.R6 [R5] — Empty lane.** A lane with no deals retains its stage heading, chance, count and total value and shows `No deals here.` in place of deal cards. Do not remove the lane or collapse its stage identity.

## Spatial behavior and states

At 1366 × 768, heading and summary are content-sized above a four-column board that takes remaining width. Stage headings stay at the top of their lanes; deal cards flow downward in stage order, without an independent vertical lane scroll. The page content owns vertical scrolling, bounded by the fixed navigation/chrome. Below 900 px, retain four readable lanes in the same order and make the board itself the localized horizontal-scroll owner; keep page vertical scroll separate. The first lane and its stage summary are visible initially, and the remaining lanes are reachable by horizontal movement. Do not shrink deal identity or actions into unreadable cards. At 375 × 812 and 320 × 720, keep the page heading and open summary above the board; horizontal board travel must not displace navigation or hide the current stage context. If the board moves horizontally, its lane content and headings move together.

## Reading, focus, and accessibility

Order: Pipeline navigation item and remaining destinations; heading; open summary; Qualified lane, then Proposal, Negotiation, Won, reading each lane’s heading/count/value and cards top-to-bottom. Within each card read deal name, company, value, owner, Back, Next. Label movement actions with the associated deal name in their accessible name; expose stage and win chance as text. Disabled boundary movement is conveyed both visually and semantically. Maintain visible focus as the board is horizontally traversed. Announce lane/summary refresh after movement; the deal-won message identifies the specific deal. Empty lanes announce `No deals here.` rather than a blank area.
