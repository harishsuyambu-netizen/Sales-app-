## Composition and regions

- **S4.R1 [R9, A1] — Persistent navigation (fixed chrome).** Use shared navigation with Contacts selected. There are no contact-create or contact-edit destinations.
- **S4.R2 [R6] — Page heading.** Show `Contacts` with subtitle `People you sell to` above the lookup task.
- **S4.R3 [R6, A7] — Search.** Place one full-width search field directly below the heading, labeled for name or company and retaining the source placeholder `Search by name or company`. Search is case-insensitive and clearing it restores the full list. At wide widths keep the field in a single row; at narrow widths it uses the full available content width.
- **S4.R4 [R6] — Contact card grid.** Place result cards immediately after search in a responsive grid. At the 1366 px reference, use three evenly sized columns for breathing room and readable contact details; each card remains at least 250 px wide. Cards are content-sized and use the shared white card treatment. Group details consistently: initials avatar with name and job title first, company next, then email, phone, and `Last contact: {relative date}`. Keep the relative recency label intact. Email and phone remain readable and reachable; allow long email strings to wrap rather than clip. This is lookup-only: do not show add, edit, or destructive actions.
- **S4.R5 [R6, A7] — No-result state.** If the case-insensitive name/company search yields no contacts, replace the grid with exact `No contacts match.` while leaving the search field available for correction.

## Spatial behavior and states

The page content owns vertical scrolling beneath fixed navigation/chrome. Search is content-sized and remains before the result grid; cards flow row-by-row and the grid expands vertically as needed. There is no independent card-grid scroll. At 1366 × 768, show three columns; at narrower widths reduce to two where each card can remain at least 250 px, then one column at the 375 × 812 and 320 × 720 anchors. Keep 26 px page inset where feasible and allow the single card column to use the available width. Below 900 px, the shared navigation is independently horizontally scrollable above the content. Do not turn the contact details into a horizontally scrolling table or hide phone/email to preserve columns.

## Reading, focus, and accessibility

Order: Contacts navigation item and remaining destinations; heading/subtitle; labeled search; cards row-by-row, each card in name, job title, company, email, phone, last-contact order. After navigation, search is the first content focus target; retain the query when returning from another area. Provide meaningful text for contact initials/name, and ensure the relative last-contact text is explicit rather than conveyed only by an icon. Use appropriate link semantics for email/phone only if the product implementation makes them actionable; do not imply creation/edit actions. Announce the empty state `No contacts match.` and retain the search query so it can be corrected.
