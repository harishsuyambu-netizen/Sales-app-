## Composition and regions

- **S5.R1 [R9, A1] — Persistent navigation (fixed chrome).** Use shared navigation with Activities selected. Keep all destinations reachable and preserve selected state.
- **S5.R2 [R8, A10, A11] — Page heading and primary action.** Show `Activities` and subtitle `Calls, meetings, emails and tasks`. Place `Add activity` at the right of the heading on wide screens; on narrow screens move it below the subtitle and before the filter/list. Activating it reveals the entry region and puts focus in the title field.
- **S5.R3 [R8, A10, A11] — Activity entry region (conditional, before list).** When open, place the form immediately below the heading and before type filters/list. Show persistent labels for What needs doing, Type, and When. Retain the source title placeholder `Follow up on proposal`. Type choices are Call, Meeting, Email, Task; due choices are Today, Tomorrow, This week. On wide screens place title across the row and type/when together below; on narrow screens stack title, type, when, validation and Save activity/Cancel in that order. Keep Save and Cancel after the fields, near the form. A valid record is incomplete and is shown for company `General` at time `Anytime`.
- **S5.R4 [R7, A8] — Type filter.** Put chips in exact order All, Calls, Meetings, Emails, Tasks above the grouped activity list. At wide widths they share a row; at narrow widths wrap in order without dropping choices. Exactly one selection is apparent. Filter type while preserving group ordering; successful save resets the filter to All.
- **S5.R5 [R7, A9] — Grouped activity list.** Display non-empty groups in Today, Tomorrow, This week order. Within each group show completion state first, then activity type, title, company, and time. Completed entries remain visible but have a muted presentation and struck-through title, along with an exposed completed state. Provide a completion action that toggles between mark complete and reopen. Keep every activity’s title/company/time visible and do not collapse group identity into color.
- **S5.R6 [R7, R8, A9–A11] — Validation, empty state, and feedback.** Empty/whitespace title on save shows exact `Describe the activity`, creates no record, and keeps the form open with focus returned to title. Valid save creates the incomplete activity with chosen type/due group, company `General`, time `Anytime`; clear title/error, close form, reset filter to All, refresh activity and dashboard information, and show `Activity added`. Cancel closes the form and clears its error without creating a record; return focus to `Add activity`. If the selected type has no matching activities, show exact `Nothing here.` and omit all empty group headings. Success feedback is the shared bottom-center transient message for about 2.2 seconds.

## Spatial behavior and states

At 1366 × 768, heading and Add activity precede the optional compact form; filter chips precede the grouped list. The grouped list is the dominant region and takes remaining page width. The page content owns all vertical scrolling, bounded by fixed navigation/chrome; activity groups do not create nested vertical scroll areas. At narrow widths, stack form fields and wrap filters, preserving order and access to Save/Cancel. Groups remain Today, Tomorrow, This week. Keep a group heading directly above its rows; when no filtered matches exist, replace the group/list region with `Nothing here.` rather than leaving empty headings. At 375 × 812 and 320 × 720, vertical scrolling is preferred over compressing the activity text or control targets.

## Reading, focus, and accessibility

Order: Activities navigation item and remaining destinations; heading/subtitle; Add activity; if open, title, type, when, validation, Save activity, Cancel; then type filters left-to-right; Today group and rows, Tomorrow group and rows, This week group and rows. Label completion actions contextually, e.g. identify the activity title and whether the action is Mark complete or Reopen. Expose completed state in text/semantics in addition to mute/strike-through, and preserve keyboard-operable completion actions with visible focus. Empty groups are omitted; announce `Nothing here.` when no results remain. Invalid save announces the inline error and focuses title; valid save/toggle announces completion feedback or changed state and refreshes the dashboard count. Cancel returns focus to the initiating Add activity action.
